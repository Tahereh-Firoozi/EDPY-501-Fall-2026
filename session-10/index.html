Study scenario

A peer-feedback guide was used in four graduate writing seminars. After revision, 32 students completed a survey. Twenty-four said they understood the feedback, and 13 said they felt confident revising. The researcher then interviewed students selected from different response patterns.

  • Student A: Understood the comment, but needed an example of how to rewrite.
  • Student B: Understood the comment, but had too little time to revise.
  • Student C: Understood the comment and used the guide to make a plan.
  • Student D: Found some comments vague, but was confident before the activity.

These are fictional teaching data. The survey reports totals, not each student's paired answers.
