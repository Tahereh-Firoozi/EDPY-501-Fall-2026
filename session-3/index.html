A few helpful research wordsOpen whenever you need a reminder
Literature review
A discussion of what earlier research tells us about a topic.
Research gap
Something we still need to understand or study.
Research design
The plan for collecting and using evidence to answer a question.
Quantitative evidence
Numbers, such as test scores or questionnaire ratings.
Qualitative evidence
Words and accounts of experiences, such as interviews or written reflections.
Mixed methods
A study that brings quantitative and qualitative evidence together.
Self-regulation / self-efficacy
Managing your own learning / believing you can carry out a task.
Limitation
A reason to be careful about how widely you apply a study’s findings.
These papers are about related ideas, but they ask different questions. Papers A and C focus on writing; Paper B focuses on science and mathematics. Noticing differences is part of a good synthesis.
